Tarjei Ellingsen Røsvoll works in administrative and constitutional law. His research concerns the legal organisation of public administration: delegation, agency steering, agencification, and the democratic legitimacy of state institutions. The work is theoretical, drawing on legal theory, organisation theory and social theory, and extends to European administrative law.

His book Delegering (Cappelen Damm Akademisk, 2025) is a critical account of how Norwegian legal scholarship understands the organisation of the administration. He holds a PhD (2023) and an integrated master’s degree in law (2019) from the University of Bergen. Before entering academia he worked as a political journalist and editor.

Research

Delegation and administrative organisation

The legal framework for how public tasks and powers are distributed within the administration, and how ministries steer the bodies they delegate to.

PhD and monograph Delegering; Jussens Venner 2020; Stat & Styring 2024

Organising public power

Institutional acts, institutional capacity and the legal construction of the state apparatus. Project Democratic legitimacy in the organisation of state power (2025–), funded by Institutt for demokrati- og legitimitetsanalyse.

ICON·S 2026, Dublin; Potentia and Public Law, Amsterdam 2026

European agencies and expertise

EU law’s limits on delegating to independent agencies, and how an administration built on expertise can be legitimated beyond the technique/politics distinction.

Lov og Rett 2025

Automation and reason-giving

Automated decision-making in public administration, the duty to give reasons, and AI in exam grading in higher education.

Input to the Malthe-Sørenssen committee 2026; public debate 2024–26

Legal history

The historical development of Norwegian legal doctrine, administrative law and the organisation of the state administration.

Tidsskrift for Rettsvitenskap 2020; master’s thesis 2019

Legal theory

Socio-legal and constitutional theory, critical legal theory, and the use of Carl Schmitt’s concepts in contemporary debate.

Teologisk tidsskrift 2023; Agora 2020, 2021; Nytt Norsk Tidsskrift 2024
